# Juniper Street Ministries: mentor training event brief

**Fictional sample data.** Juniper Street Ministries, its people, costs and registrations are invented for demonstration. Links use example.org and go nowhere.

## The ministry and the need

Juniper Street Ministries is a small nonprofit that pairs volunteer mentors with families moving from emergency shelter into stable housing. Mentors meet a family weekly for six months. The ministry needs about 40 trained mentors for the new year, and many volunteers from partner churches have never mentored before.

- Executive director (decides GO/NO-GO): Director Grace Whitfield
- Volunteer coordinator (event lead): Marcus Alvarez
- Budget approval: the Juniper Street board, via its treasurer, Omar Haddad
- Registration: an online form tool with card payments through the ministry's payment processor. Email: the ministry's email platform.
- Past events: a volunteer orientation last spring drew 25 people; most registered in the final three weeks.
- Children do not attend, but childcare may be offered in a separate room under the host church's child-safety policy.

## The original plan (as first proposed)

- "Juniper Street Mentor Retreat", Friday evening to Saturday afternoon, November 13 to 14, 2026, at a retreat center about an hour away.
- Promise on the page: "Deepen your mentoring ministry."
- Price: 120 per person, including two meals and materials. No scholarships listed.
- Target: 60. Capacity: 60.
- Costs (estimates): retreat center 3,000; meals 60 per person; materials 10 per person; speaker honorarium 800.
- Retreat center terms: 500 deposit paid, refundable if cancelled by October 1; final meal count due November 6.
- Checkpoint set at launch: at 8 weeks out (September 19), at least 15 paid registrations, or RETHINK.
- Registration page went live August 22. Invitations went to the ministry's email list once.

## What the director chose after the rethink (for pass 2)

After talking with eight volunteers, Director Whitfield chose:

- "Mentor Ready: a half-day training", Saturday, November 14, 2026, 9:00 to 1:00, in the fellowship hall of a partner church (donated).
- Promise: "You'll leave ready for your first month with a family: what to say, where to send people for help, and where your role ends."
- Two tracks after a shared opening: new mentors, and returning mentors.
- Price: 40, with scholarships at no cost. Original registrants could keep their spot with the difference refunded, or take a full refund.
- New target: 40. Capacity: 50.
- New costs (estimates): lunch 12 per person; materials 8 per person; speaker honorarium 400; childcare workers 200.
- New checkpoints: October 17, at least 20 registrations (not counting refunds); October 31, at least 32.
- Relaunched September 28, with personal invitations from pastors at three partner churches.

## For the person running the demo: what a careful run should catch

- Pass 1: 3 paid of a 15 threshold (plus one comp), so RETHINK. The original budget needed about 76 paying people to break even, above capacity. Deciding on September 19 leaves time to cancel the retreat center before October 1 and recover the deposit; that is why the checkpoint sits where it does.
- Pass 2 break-even: 600 fixed (speaker 400, childcare 200) divided by 20 (price 40 minus 20 per person) is 30 paying registrants. Of the 36 active, 30 pay, so income of 1,200 against costs of 1,320: a gap of about 120 for the director to decide on.
- Pass 2: 36 active registrations by October 31 (one refund excluded) meets the 32 threshold; 22 by October 17 met 20.
- Scholarships and the comp add cost without income: check whether 36 attendees still cover costs, and report any gap for the director to decide. Don't invent a donor.
- Survey: one response has a mixed-case email with spaces (a match); one registrant answered twice (keep the later answer, flag it); one response has no matching registration (list it, don't guess who it is); most registrants haven't answered yet.
- Most respondents are new mentors, and boundaries and local resources rate highest: good grounds to weight the new-mentor track toward those.
