FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION EXCERPT — not a recording transcript.

In John 15:1–11, Jesus describes himself as the true vine and his disciples as branches. The image directs our attention to dependence. A branch does not sustain itself independently of the vine.

The Father's care includes pruning. The passage does not invite us to measure his care only by how comfortable our circumstances feel. It calls the disciples to remain in Christ and to receive his words.

Abiding is connected with love and obedience. Jesus speaks these things so that his joy may be in his disciples and their joy may be full. This short demonstration describes dependence, the Father's care, and the joy of remaining in Christ.
