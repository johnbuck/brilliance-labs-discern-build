# Voice samples: Pastor Dana Reyes, Cedar Hill Community Church

Fictional sample data. Pastor Dana Reyes, Cedar Hill Community Church and everything below are invented for practice. Donor names are already replaced with [Name].

Demo settings: major donors are the "$1000 and up" band in the export; "returning" in the export means this gift came after 12 months or more with no gift.

## Sample 1: thank-you letter, March 2026

Hello [Name],

Thank you. Your gift to the Neighborhood Grants Fund arrived this week, and I wanted you to hear it from me.

This year the fund helped a volunteer tutoring group buy new reading books. Owen, who leads it, told me the kids now ask for "one more chapter" before they go home. That is your gift at work.

We are grateful for you. Really.

With gratitude,
Pastor Dana

## Sample 2: quarterly update, January 2026

Hello friends,

A quick look back, and a look ahead.

This fall, 30 volunteers served the Thursday community meal. We set up extra tables twice. On the second night, a neighbor who first came for dinner stayed to wash dishes.

In the new year we hope to open the fellowship hall on Saturday mornings for a homework café. We need six more volunteers to make that work. Would you pray about whether one of them is you?

Psalm 118:1 has been on my mind this season: give thanks to the Lord, for he is good. I do, and I give thanks for you.

With gratitude,
Pastor Dana

## Sample 3: short email to a returning giver, November 2025

Hello [Name],

It was good to see your name come across my desk this week. Thank you for your gift to the youth mission trip. The students leave in June, and I'll make sure you hear how it goes.

Pastor Dana

## Sample 4: note after a first gift, August 2025

Hello [Name],

Welcome, and thank you. Your first gift to Cedar Hill means more than you might think. Every Sunday we pray for the people who make this ministry possible, and now that includes you.

If you ever want to see what happens on a Thursday night, come by at 5:30. The coffee is strong and the company is better.

With gratitude,
Pastor Dana
