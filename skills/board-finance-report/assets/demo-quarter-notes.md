# Fictional sample data: Cedar Hill Community Church, first quarter 2026

Invented for practice. It stands in for a QuickBooks Online Balance Sheet export and the treasurer's notes. Use it with `demo-actuals.csv`, `demo-budget.csv` and `demo-variance-notes.csv`.

## Books status

Books closed and reconciled through March 31, 2026 by the church administrator, reviewed by Treasurer Sam Okafor. Basis: modified cash, as recorded by the board (cash basis, except that investments are carried at market value and payroll taxes withheld but not yet paid are shown as a liability).

## Balance sheet summary, March 31, 2026

| Line | Dec 31, 2025 | Mar 31, 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Checking | 24,000 | 27,215 |
| Savings (operating reserve) | 40,000 | 42,000 |
| Reserve investment account, market value | 49,000 | 51,300 |
| Total assets | 113,000 | 120,515 |
| Liabilities (payroll taxes in transit) | 1,000 | 700 |
| Net assets | 112,000 | 119,815 |

Net assets changed by 7,815 in the quarter: the 5,515 operating result plus 2,300 of net investment return (dividends less fees plus the change in market value, mostly unrealized and not cash), which the board sees on its own line below the operating result. The operating lines in `demo-actuals.csv` exclude the investment account; "Interest" there is checking and savings interest only.

## Cash policy

Board policy keeps at least 20,000 in checking and savings combined that is neither donor-restricted nor board-designated. Available cash at March 31: checking 27,215 plus savings 42,000, less 4,100 of restricted funds, less the 30,000 building reserve held in savings = 35,115.

## For the board to decide

The sound equipment (1,500) was not in the budget. Pastor Dana Reyes asks the board to ratify the purchase and to decide whether future unbudgeted purchases over 1,000 need approval before they are made.
