# Fictional sample data: Cedar Hill Community Church 2027 budget planning notes

Invented for practice. These stand in for the notes from a planning conversation with Pastor Dana Reyes and Treasurer Sam Okafor. Use them with `demo-actuals-and-forecast.csv`.

## Calendar

- Fiscal year: January to December. Budget year: 2027.
- Finance committee reviews the draft in October 2026. The board votes at its November 2026 meeting.

## What leadership wants the year to do

- Keep current ministries steady.
- Add a part-time youth director from March 2027, if the board agrees.
- Keep missions giving at the level members have designated.

## Cash and reserves

- Estimated unrestricted cash on January 1, 2027: 30,000 (true up after the December close).
- Cash floor: keep at least 20,000 of unrestricted cash at every month end.
- Savings: 42,000, of which 30,000 is the board-designated building reserve and 12,000 is operating reserve. Proposed draw limit: up to 12,000 in 2027 from the operating reserve, taken only if needed to hold the cash floor. A backstop, not income.

## Monthly giving pattern (share of the year's tithes and online giving)

Jan 7%, Feb 7%, Mar 8%, Apr 9%, May 8%, Jun 7%, Jul 6%, Aug 6%, Sep 8%, Oct 8%, Nov 9%, Dec 17%. Expenses are roughly even by month, except insurance paid in January.

## Treasurer's first-round questions on the draft (fictional)

1. "What does the year look like if we don't hire the youth director?"
2. "Is 3% the latest inflation figure? Cite the source and date."
3. "Which month is cash lowest, and does it stay above the floor in the downside case?"
