# Research Summary Template

## Format guidance

**When to use**: general research requests — the most common format and the default when no other fits. See [format-selection.md](../format-selection.md) for the full decision tree.

**Length**: 500-1000 words typically.

**Structure**: executive summary (2-3 sentences), 3-5 key findings with supporting evidence, detailed analysis section, conclusions and next steps, source citations.

**Best for**:

- Most general-purpose research requests
- Standard documentation needs
- Balanced depth and readability
- When you need comprehensive but accessible information

**Example prompts**:

- "Research our authentication options"
- "What does our project documentation say about the API redesign?"
- "Compile information about our deployment process"
- "Summarize the state of mobile strategy discussions"
