# Quick Brief Template

## Format guidance

**When to use**: time-sensitive requests, simple topics, status updates, quick reference needs — when time matters more than depth. See [format-selection.md](../format-selection.md) for the full decision tree.

**Length**: 200-400 words.

**Structure**: 3-4 sentence summary, 3-5 bullet key points, short action items list, brief source list.

**Best for**:

- Fast turnaround requests
- Simple, straightforward topics
- Quick status updates
- Initial exploration before deeper research

**Example prompts**:

- "Quick summary of what's in our API docs"
- "What are the key points from that spec?"
- "Give me a quick overview of the project status"
- "Fast brief on where the migration stands"

## Skeleton

```markdown
# [Topic] - Quick Brief

**Date**: [Current date]

## Summary
[3-4 sentences covering the essentials]

## Key Points
- **Point 1**: [Details] ([Source Title](url))
- **Point 2**: [Details] ([Source Title](url))
- **Point 3**: [Details] ([Source Title](url))

## Action Items
1. [Immediate next step]
2. [Follow-up action]

## Sources
- [Source Title](url) [E:id — where support is not evident from the link alone]
```
