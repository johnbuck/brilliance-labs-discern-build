# Cedar Hill Neighborhood Grants Fund: review rubric

Fictional sample data. Cedar Hill Community Church and its fund are invented for practice.

## About the fund

Cedar Hill Community Church sets aside money each year to support small ministries serving our neighborhood. Grants range from $1,000 to $5,000. We fund two rounds a year, about $10,000 per round. Grants coordinator: Pastor Dana Reyes. Treasurer: Sam Okafor. The missions committee (five members) reviews and decides.

## How reviewers score

Score each criterion 1, 2 or 3. Write one sentence of reasoning for each score, pointing to the application.

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neighborhood need | Need is unclear | Need is described | Need is described with specifics (who, how many, why now) |
| Fit with the fund | Outside our purpose | Partly fits | Clearly serves neighbors in the fund's area |
| Clear, realistic plan | Vague | Mostly clear | Clear steps, timeline and budget that match the request |
| Relationships and people | No named leaders or partners | Named leaders | Named leaders with local relationships and volunteers |
| Faithful follow-through | New or no track record, no reporting plan | Some track record or a reporting plan | Past grants reported on time, or a clear reporting plan |

Total: 5 to 15. Scores guide the conversation; the committee decides.

## Conflicts of interest

A reviewer who leads, serves on the board of, or has family in an applicant ministry tells the chair and does not score that application.

## Impact report (due six months after the award)

- What you did with the grant, in a few sentences
- About how many people took part
- One story or quote you are glad to share (say whether we may share it publicly)
- Receipts or a simple spending summary
- Photos, if you have permission from those pictured
