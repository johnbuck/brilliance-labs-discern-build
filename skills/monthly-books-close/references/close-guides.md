# Close guides, checklist and review memo

Templates for the files this skill writes. Keep each one to about a page, in plain words a new volunteer could follow. Fill every bracket from the ministry's own answers and statements; leave a bracket visible and list it as an open question if you don't know.

## One close guide per account

File: `[order]. [Account nickname] close guide-Claude.md`. The treasurer approves it before first use.

1. **What this account is:** purpose, QuickBooks Online account name, bank feed yes or no, which statement to download and where to save it.
2. **Quick checklist:** five to eight lines, in order, each a single action.
3. **How to handle each kind of transaction:** a table with columns "Looks like", "Where it goes (account and class)", "Approved as routine?" Include transfers, recurring bills, deposits, checks, fees and the rule "unknown: hold and ask".
4. **Journal entries this account needs each month (if any):** name pattern (for example `Invest Unreal YY-MM`, 21 characters or fewer), which lines, and where each number comes from on the statement.
5. **How to reconcile:** the statement figure to enter and what a 0.00 difference means.
6. **Common problems:** duplicates, transfers seen on both sides, missing check payee, a rule that picked the wrong class.
7. **Change log:** date and what the treasurer decided.

## Reconciliation checklist (one per month)

A table: account, statement ending balance, QuickBooks Online balance after entries, difference, reconciled (yes or no), who reconciled, open items. Add rows for holding accounts that should be zero at month end (for example Uncategorized Expense or an online-giving clearing account) and whether each is zero. End with one line: closing date set in QuickBooks Online through this month, yes or no, and by whom.

## Treasurer review memo

File: `YYYY-MM Close review memo-Claude.md` (or .docx). One to two pages. Covering several months at once is fine during a catch-up.

1. **Summary:** which months are closed, the accounting basis the books use, whether every account reconciled to 0.00, whether holding accounts are zero, and one sentence on how the work was done ("closed by [role] with Claude drafting from statements; nothing posted without a matching statement figure").
2. **What was done, by account:** short bullets of anything beyond routine.
3. **Verification table:** the reconciliation checklist figures.
4. **Decisions that need your sign-off:** new categorizations, allocation changes, corrections to prior months, anything over the flag threshold. One bullet each, with the recommendation and the reason.
5. **Items for your review:** open questions, uncleared items, anything you could not explain.
6. **How to review:** which files to open and which QuickBooks Online reports reproduce the figures (Balance Sheet and Profit and Loss for the month, Profit and Loss by Class, reconciliation reports).
7. **Process changes:** any guide you updated and why.

Write figures only from statements and QuickBooks reports. Refer to donors by gift ID or initials unless the profile says names are fine.
