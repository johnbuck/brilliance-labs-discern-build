# Minutes format

A common form for ministry boards and committees. The user's approved reference minutes win where they differ. Minutes record actions and decisions, with brief summaries of discussion only if the body wants them.

```
DRAFT: not approved

[Ministry name]
Minutes of the [body name], [regular / special] meeting
[Date], [start time] [time zone], [place, or "by video call"]

Present: [voting members present]
Absent: [voting members absent]
Also present: [staff, guests]
Presiding: [name, role]. Minutes taken by: [name, role].

1. Call to order. [Name] called the meeting to order at [time].
   Quorum: [N] of [M] voting members present; a quorum [was / was not] present.
   (Or: "Quorum: to confirm.")
2. Opening prayer or devotion. [Only if the notes show it; name only if stated.]
3. Approval of the agenda. [Motion, or "approved by consensus", as the notes show.]
4. Approval of prior minutes. [Minutes of (date) approved (as presented / with corrections).]
5. Reports. [Report title], presented by [name]. [One or two sentences.] Received.
6. Old business. [Item: what was decided, or "discussed; no action taken".]
7. New business.
   MOTION: [wording as stated]. Moved by [name]; seconded by [name].
   Vote: [carried / failed], [counts or "by voice vote"]. [Abstentions if recorded.]
8. Executive session. [Only in the wording the user approved, for example
   "The board met in executive session from [time] to [time]. No action was taken."]
9. Adjournment. The meeting adjourned at [time].

Submitted by [name, role], [date]
Approved: ________ (date of approval)
```

Rules:
- Use the motion's wording as stated. Don't polish it into something the body didn't vote on. If wording was unclear, keep the closest version and flag it.
- Robert's Rules does not require the seconder's name or a count of abstentions in minutes, but many church boards record both. Follow the reference minutes; when in doubt, record what the notes show and let the clerk cut.
- Minutes belong to the body, not the secretary: they stay DRAFT until the body approves them at a later meeting, and the approval (with any corrections) is recorded in that meeting's minutes.
- Use "[not in notes: confirm]" for any missing mover, seconder, count or time.
- Items discussed without a vote say "discussed; no action taken". Items postponed say "tabled" or "referred to [group]" only if the notes use those terms.
- A motion that was withdrawn or had no second is recorded only as the notes show it.
- The action list lives in a separate document, not inside the minutes, unless the reference minutes include one.
