# Do Justly, Love Mercy

Fictional sample data. A short, made-up sermon excerpt for practice; not a real transcript. Scripture quotations are from the King James Version (public domain).

- Church: Cedar Hill Community Church
- Date: 2026-05-17
- Speaker: Pastor Dana Reyes
- Passage: Micah 6:6-8
- Series: Love in Action

Micah imagines someone asking what would be enough to please God. Burnt offerings? Calves a year old? Thousands of rams? Even a firstborn child? Each offer is bigger than the last, as if God could be bought.

Micah's answer is simple and hard. "He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth the LORD require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God?"

To do justly is to treat people fairly, especially people with less power. To love mercy is not just to show kindness now and then, but to delight in it. To walk humbly is to remember that we follow God; we do not lead him.

The prophets keep these together. Amos 5:24 says, "let judgment run down as waters, and righteousness as a mighty stream." Worship that ignores our neighbors is not what God asked for.

What does that look like at Cedar Hill? Paying our pantry suppliers on time. Treating every guest in the Saturday line with dignity, the same way we would treat a guest on Sunday. Speaking up when someone is treated unfairly. None of this earns God's love. It is what his love looks like when it takes hold of a church.
