# Who Is My Neighbor?

Fictional sample data. A short, made-up sermon excerpt for practice; not a real transcript. Scripture quotations are from the King James Version (public domain).

- Church: Cedar Hill Community Church
- Date: 2026-02-08
- Speaker: Associate Pastor Priya Nair
- Passage: Luke 10:25-37
- Series: Love in Action

A lawyer asks Jesus what he must do to inherit eternal life. Jesus asks him what the law says, and he answers well: love God with all your heart, soul, strength and mind, and love your neighbor as yourself. That joins Deuteronomy 6:5 and Leviticus 19:18. Then he asks the question behind the question: who is my neighbor?

Jesus answers with a story. A man going down from Jerusalem to Jericho is robbed and left half dead. A priest sees him and passes by on the other side. So does a Levite. Then a Samaritan, someone the listeners would have looked down on, sees him and has compassion.

The Samaritan does not just feel sorry. He binds up the wounds, pours in oil and wine, puts the man on his own animal, takes him to an inn, and pays for his care. Compassion costs him time, money and his plans for the day.

Then Jesus turns the question around. He does not ask who deserved help. He asks which one was a neighbor to the man. And he says, "Go, and do thou likewise."

For us, the road to Jericho might be the Saturday line at our food pantry, or the family next door who just moved here and knows no one. Mercy crosses the lines we draw. This month, pray for one person you usually pass by, and then go and meet them.
