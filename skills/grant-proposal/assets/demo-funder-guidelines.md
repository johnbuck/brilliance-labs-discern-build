# Lantern Rock Foundation: Community Flourishing Grants

Fictional sample data. This foundation, its staff and its guidelines are invented for practice. No real funder is described.

Website: https://example.org/lantern-rock/grants
Retrieved: September 2026

## Who we fund

The Lantern Rock Foundation supports faith-based and community nonprofits that help children and families in Lantern County thrive. We give priority to programs that:

- serve families with limited income,
- partner with local schools, churches or neighborhood groups,
- measure results and share what they learn.

## Eligibility

- IRS 501(c)(3) public charity, or a church with documentation of tax-exempt status
- Operating for at least three years
- Annual operating budget under $2,000,000
- Serving residents of Lantern County

We do not fund capital campaigns, debt reduction, political activity or individuals.

## Grant size and timing

- Grants from $10,000 to $40,000 for one year
- Letter of intent (LOI) deadline: November 13, 2026, 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time
- Invited full proposals due: February 12, 2027
- Decisions: May 2027. Grant period begins July 1, 2027.

## Letter of intent questions (online form)

The form accepts plain text only. Tables and images are not accepted.

1. Amount requested (number)
2. Total project cost (number)
3. Project start and end dates
4. Organization summary: who you are and whom you serve (150 words)
5. Project description: what you will do, with whom, and why it is needed (300 words)
6. Expected outcomes: what will be different, and how you will measure it (200 words)
7. How Lantern Rock funds will be used (150 words)
8. Have you contacted Lantern Rock staff about this project? If so, describe. (100 words)

## Budget rules

Indirect costs up to 10 percent of direct costs are allowed. We fund up to 60 percent of a project's total cost; please show other sources.

## If invited to a full proposal

Attach: IRS determination letter or church documentation, board list with affiliations, most recent financial statements or audit, project budget, organizational budget, and up to two letters of support.

## Reporting

Awarded grants submit a six-month interim report and a final report within 30 days of the grant period's end.
