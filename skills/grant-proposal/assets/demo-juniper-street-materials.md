# Juniper Street Ministries: materials for a grant application

Fictional sample data. Juniper Street Ministries, its people, numbers and partners are invented for practice.

## About us (from our annual report, fiscal year ending June 2026)

Juniper Street Ministries is a Christian nonprofit in Lantern County. We began in 2016 as a neighborhood outreach of Cedar Hill Community Church and became our own 501(c)(3) in 2018. We walk with families in the Juniper Street neighborhood through after-school tutoring, weekly family dinners and a small emergency food shelf. Our motto: "Every child known, every family welcomed."

- Status: 501(c)(3) public charity; IRS determination letter on file
- Annual operating budget: about $600,000
- Staff: 6 full-time, 3 part-time; about 80 active volunteers
- Board: 9 members; chair Grace Lin
- Executive director: Alex Moreno. Treasurer: Sam Okafor.

## Current program: the Juniper Homework Club

The Juniper Homework Club is our after-school program for middle school students, Monday to Thursday, 3:30 to 5:30 p.m., at our building on Juniper Street. Volunteer tutors help with homework and reading. On Thursdays, families join for dinner.

## Outcomes from 2025-26 (source: attendance log and spring teacher survey, compiled by program staff, July 2026)

- 40 students enrolled during the school year
- 32 of 40 attended at least 70 percent of sessions
- Teachers reported better homework completion for 25 of the 31 students they rated
- 28 families came to at least one Thursday dinner
- Reading-level change: not measured this year

## Proposed expansion for 2027-28

Grow the Juniper Homework Club from 40 to 60 students, add a Wednesday family dinner, and hire a part-time program coordinator so volunteers have steady support.

Partner note from the executive director: "The Lantern Middle School principal said she would like a referral agreement with us. Nothing is signed yet, and we haven't discussed details."
