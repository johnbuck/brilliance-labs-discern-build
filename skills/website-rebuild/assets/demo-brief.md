# Cedar Hill Community Church: website brief

Fictional sample data. Cedar Hill Community Church, its people, address and website are made up for practice. Use `example.org` links only.

## Notes from the first planning conversation

- Approver: Pastor Dana Reyes (lead pastor). Final say on words and design.
- Domain account: Church Administrator Morgan Ellis controls it. Morgan will make any DNS change.
- Current site: https://www.example.org, on a template site builder, set up about eight years ago by a volunteer who has since moved away.
- Size: about 300 people on a typical Sunday.
- Maintainer after launch: Morgan, a few hours a month. Comfortable with email and spreadsheets, not with code.
- Timeline: launch before the fall kickoff, about six weeks from now.
- Data sensitivity answer: "Some". The prayer request form and the youth sign-up form collect personal details; do not look at submissions.
- The companion file `demo-site-inventory.csv` is the raw walk of the current site; step 2 adds the keep/merge/drop, new URL and redirect columns.

## Audiences, most important first

1. First-time visitors who found us online and want to know what Sunday is like.
2. Families with children who want to know about kids' programs and safety.
3. Neighbors looking for help from the food pantry.

## What a first-time visitor should be able to do within a minute

- Find service times and the address.
- Know what to expect (what to wear, where to park, what happens with kids).
- Find the food pantry hours.

## Voice

- Sounds like: warm, plain, a little funny, neighborly. "Come as you are" is how people describe us.
- Avoid: church jargon without explanation, "life-changing", "vibrant", "do life together".
- Reference: Pastor Dana's welcome letter in last fall's newsletter (not included in this demo).

## Never change without asking

- Mission statement (exact text): "Cedar Hill Community Church exists to love God, love our neighbors, and make room at the table for everyone."
- Statement of faith: keep the current wording; link to it, don't rewrite it.
- The name "Cedar Hill Community Church" (never shortened to "CHCC" in headings).

## Confirmed facts

- Sunday services: 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM.
- Address: 100 Example Street, Springfield.
- Phone: (555) 555-0100. Email: hello@example.org.
- Food pantry: Saturdays, 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, in the fellowship hall.

## Not yet confirmed (use placeholders)

- Kids' check-in process and which services have children's programs.
- Parking details.
- Current staff list (two people listed on the old site have left).

## Must keep

- Sermon archive (audio, about five years).
- Online giving link (goes to the church's payment processor).
- Prayer request form and youth event sign-up form.
- The monthly newsletter PDF that is linked from emails.
