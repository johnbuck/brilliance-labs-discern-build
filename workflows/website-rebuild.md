# Rebuild your ministry website with Claude

## What this helps you do

Plan, write and launch a clearer ministry website, with Claude doing most of the drafting and building. You make every decision, review a preview link before anything goes live, and keep your old site until the new one works.

## Who it's for

Pastors, directors, administrators and volunteers whose site is outdated or stuck on a template site builder (for example Squarespace or Wix). No coding needed.

## What you'll need

- Claude (Claude Desktop is best; it can work in your files), your web address, and writing that sounds like you.
- Who approves words and design, and who controls your domain (your web address account).
- Time: about 45 minutes to start, a few sessions for writing and mockups (sample page designs), and about a month to launch.

## The steps

1. Tell Claude your goal, who the site is for, and what a first-time visitor should do within a minute. Share your files.
2. Claude lists every page and form on your current site, so nothing gets lost.
3. Write down the words that must never change without your say-so, such as your mission statement.
4. Agree on a short page list, then choose from tagline and homepage options, each with reasons and risks.
5. Claude drafts page copy in your voice and checks it before you review it.
6. Review clickable mockups of every page on a phone and a computer.
7. Keep your site builder or let Claude build a simple site; review it at a preview link.
8. Launch with a checklist: forms tested, old links redirected, privacy notice posted, phone and accessibility checks done. Your domain owner makes the switch.

## What stays with people

Your approver chooses the tagline, approves every page and says when to launch. Your domain owner makes any domain change. A leader reviews the privacy notice.

## Keep it safe

Share what your forms ask, never what people submitted: no prayer requests, member lists or giving records. Use only photos you have permission for, especially of children. Never paste passwords into a chat.

## Try it

"I want to rebuild our church website, [web address]. It mainly serves [audiences], and a first-time visitor should be able to [goal]. Our mission statement is [exact text]; never change it without asking. Lead me one step at a time: first list every page and form on our current site, then propose a short page list. Ask me for facts; don't invent any."

## Use the skill

Download the `website-rebuild` skill from https://github.com/johnbuck/brilliance-labs-discern-build and upload it in Claude's skills settings (Settings › Capabilities › Skills in Claude Desktop or claude.ai).
