# Micro-grant program

## What this helps you do

Run a fund that gives small grants to other ministries with far less paperwork, so staff and board spend their time with the people you fund. The church at Antioch did this when it sent relief to believers in Judea through trusted hands (Acts 11:29-30).

## Who it's for

Grants coordinators, pastors, outreach or missions committees, administrators and treasurers at a church or ministry that gives small grants.

## What you'll need

- An application form (for example a Google Form or your website's form builder)
- A spreadsheet for your master record: the one list of every application, award and report
- Past spreadsheets, form responses or emails about earlier grants
- Scoring criteria (a rubric), or 15 minutes to draft one
- About an hour to set up, then 20 to 30 minutes per round plus committee review

## The steps

1. Draft a short application form whose questions match your master record's columns.
2. Combine old spreadsheets into one master record (on backup copies), linking each award to its application and each report to its award.
3. When a round closes, the AI writes a same-length summary of each application against your rubric, without contact details, plus a score sheet.
4. After your committee decides, the AI drafts award and decline letters and an announcement for you to review and send.
5. Monthly, check who owes an impact report, draft friendly reminders, and log reports as they arrive.
6. Keep a snapshot: summary, organizations, grants awarded, impact reports.
7. Yearly, build an impact story: a full edition for leaders, a shareable one for the congregation.

## What stays with people

Scoring, awards, what letters say, payments and what is shared publicly. Your committee decides; your treasurer pays. The AI doesn't rank applicants unless asked, and never touches bank details.

## Keep it safe

Keep emails and phone numbers away from reviewers and the AI; use an application number. Share grantee stories and photos only with permission. Back up spreadsheets before big changes.
