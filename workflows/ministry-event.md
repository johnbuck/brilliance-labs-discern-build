# Plan and run a ministry event

## What this helps you do

Plan a retreat, training or outreach day from first idea to follow-up. You set "are we on track?" checkpoints, shape the day around registrants' needs, and change course early if sign-ups lag.

## Who it's for

Pastors, ministry directors and volunteers organizing an event, free or paid.

## What you'll need

- An AI assistant such as Claude; Claude Desktop can work in your event folder.
- Your idea, audience, date options, real costs, venue terms and any past attendance.
- A registration tool that can export sign-ups as a spreadsheet.
- About an hour for the first plan, then 10 to 15 minutes per checkpoint.

## The steps

1. Describe the event, who it's for, and what should be different for them afterward.
2. Turn that into one clear promise ("You'll leave with ...") and a format and schedule that fit.
3. Build the budget from real costs and see how many people each price option needs. Your approver signs off.
4. Before promoting, set 3 or 4 dated checkpoints with numbers, the last one before your venue's cancellation deadline.
5. Draft the event and registration pages, an "is this for me?" guide, the invitation email and social posts.
6. At each checkpoint, share the sign-up export. If you're behind, rethink: a shorter event, a clearer promise, a different price or personal invitations often beat more promotion.
7. Send registrants a 5-minute survey, match answers to registrations, and adjust the day to their needs.
8. Finish with a day-of run sheet, a short feedback survey, and a recap with real numbers.

## What stays with people

Approving the budget, every go or no-go call, publishing, sending, posting and spending. The AI drafts and analyzes; your leaders decide.

## Keep it safe

For checkpoints, share counts and dates, not names. For survey matching, keep only email or registration number. Follow your child-safety policy if children attend. Quote attendees only with permission.

## Try it

Copy this into any AI chat:

> I'm planning [event idea] for [audience] at [ministry name], around [date]. Help me plan it end to end, one question at a time. Start with a one-sentence promise of what people will leave with, then a format, a budget from my real costs (never invent numbers), and 3 or 4 dated GO/NO-GO checkpoints with registration targets. Then draft the registration page, invitation email, short pre-event survey and day-of run sheet. Everything is a draft; I'll review and send.
