# Turn a meeting into minutes and action

## What this helps you do

Turn meeting notes into draft minutes, a to-do list by person, and follow-up emails in one pass. You review everything before it goes anywhere.

## Who it's for

Board secretaries, church clerks, administrators and pastors who take meeting notes.

## What you'll need

- An AI assistant such as Claude. Claude Desktop can read notes straight from your meetings folder.
- Your notes (an export from your note app, a typed document, or a phone photo of handwriting) plus the agenda.
- Your quorum rule (how many voting members must be present) and one set of past minutes you like.
- About 20 minutes the first time, 5 to 10 after.

## The steps

1. Share your notes, the agenda and the date, and ask for minutes, an action list and follow-up drafts together.
2. First, the AI lists anything confidential: executive session (the private part of a meeting), pastoral matters, personnel. You decide what stays out.
3. It lists the facts: attendance, quorum, each motion with mover, seconder and vote, and each task with owner and due date. Anything missing is marked "confirm", never guessed.
4. It drafts the minutes (marked DRAFT), the action list and the emails.
5. It re-checks its work against your notes; then you and your chair review line by line.
6. A person puts the minutes in the board packet and sends the emails.
7. When the board approves the minutes at its next meeting, the DRAFT label comes off.

## What stays with people

Deciding what is confidential, confirming every motion and vote, approving the minutes, and sending anything. The AI never sends email.

## Keep it safe

Remove executive-session and pastoral sections before sharing if you can. Prayer requests and staff matters don't belong in minutes or group emails. Check your own rules on recording meetings and keeping recordings.

## Try it

Copy this into any AI chat, then paste or attach your notes:

> Here are the notes from our [board / committee] meeting on [date]. Our quorum rule is: [rule]. Do three things in one pass: draft minutes (attendance, quorum, each motion with mover, seconder and result, decisions), an action list grouped by owner with due dates, and short follow-up email drafts. First, list anything that looks like executive session, pastoral or personnel content and ask me before using it. Never invent a motion, vote, attendee or date; mark anything missing "confirm". Label the minutes DRAFT.
