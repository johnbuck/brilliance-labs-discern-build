# Monthly books close

## What this helps you do

Close your ministry's books in QuickBooks Online each month, even if you have never done bookkeeping. Claude reads your statements, drafts the entries and a short memo, and your treasurer reviews before anything is posted.

## Who it's for

A pastor, director or volunteer who inherited the books, often after a bookkeeper left, and the treasurer who checks the work.

## What you'll need

- QuickBooks Online, and someone who can import files into it.
- Each month's statements (PDF or CSV) for every account.
- A treasurer or other reviewer.
- Time: 1 to 2 hours to set up, 2 to 3 hours for the first month, about an hour after that.

## The steps

1. List every account the ministry has, then write a one-page close guide for each with Claude. Your treasurer approves the guides.
2. Each month, download the statements into one shared folder.
3. Work the bank feed (where QuickBooks shows new bank transactions) with Claude coaching. Hold anything you don't recognize.
4. Claude drafts journal entries (the adjustments a bank feed can't make, like payroll or investment changes) and marks anything it's unsure of with FLAG.
5. You and the treasurer answer each flag in a review copy. Claude works in every answer.
6. Claude builds a clean upload file and checks that every entry balances. The treasurer approves it.
7. You import the file into QuickBooks and reconcile each account (match QuickBooks to the statement) until the difference is zero.
8. Claude writes a short review memo for the treasurer and updates a handoff note for next month.

## What stays with people

Approving every entry before it's posted. Deciding anything unclear, like an unknown charge or which fund a gift belongs to. Calling a CPA (an accountant) when needed, and locking the month once approved.

## Keep it safe

Never share bank or QuickBooks passwords with any AI. Refer to donors by initials or gift number, and keep full account numbers out of notes. Practice on a sample month first.

## Try it

Copy this into any AI chat:

> I help run a small ministry and I'm now responsible for closing the books in QuickBooks Online, with our treasurer reviewing. I have no accounting background. Ask me one question at a time to list our accounts, then help me write a one-page monthly close guide for our checking account. Don't guess at anything; mark unclear items for the treasurer. Explain terms as you go.
