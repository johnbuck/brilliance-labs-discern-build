# Donor follow-up

## What this helps you do

Thank donors personally and send real updates in your own voice, without spending every evening on letters. Paul's thanks to the Philippians for their gift (Philippians 4:10-20) shows that gratitude is ministry, not paperwork.

## Who it's for

Pastors, executive directors, development staff and the administrators who prepare letters for them.

## What you'll need

- 5 to 10 letters or emails the leader wrote to supporters, with donor names removed
- A giving export (a spreadsheet from your giving system) with only a few columns
- About 30 minutes to build a voice profile, then about 15 minutes per batch plus review

## The steps

1. Share past letters so the AI builds a short voice profile: sentence length, greetings, warmth, faith language, phrases to avoid. Build it once; reuse it.
2. Test it on one sample letter and tell the AI what sounds wrong.
3. Export only first name, gift date, fund, a gift range (like "under $250") and first-time, recurring or returning.
4. The AI drafts one thank-you per group in your voice, personalized with first name and fund.
5. Review every draft in a simple sheet (send, edit or redo). Your edits improve the next batch.
6. Get a call list for major donors, with talking points, so the leader can thank them in person.
7. Quarterly, give the AI real stories for an update, and keep a plan per major donor: know, grow, invite when ready, thank.

## What stays with people

Who gets a call, what letters say, when to ask for a gift, and pressing send. The leader reviews every letter; you send from your own system.

## Keep it safe

Giving is confidential, and some churches keep it from the pastor on purpose; follow your policy on who sees it. Share the fewest columns you can: no addresses, emails, phones, notes or exact amounts. Under a strict policy, ask for [First name] templates and fill in the names yourself. Delete working exports when done. Tax receipts still come from your giving system or treasurer.

## Try it

Copy this into any AI chat and paste two or three past letters (names removed):
