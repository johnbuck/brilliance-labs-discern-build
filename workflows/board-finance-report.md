# Board finance report

## What this helps you do

Turn your QuickBooks Online reports into a short financial packet your board can read in ten minutes: a one-page summary, a budget-versus-actual table, simple charts and an appendix. Your treasurer reviews each draft first.

## Who it's for

Treasurers, executive directors, pastors and administrators who report finances to a board, finance committee or elders.

## What you'll need

- Books closed and reconciled for the period.
- QuickBooks Online reports for the period: Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, and Budget vs. Actuals (or your budget spreadsheet).
- A treasurer or other reviewer.
- Time: about 2 hours for the first packet plus the review; about 45 minutes after that.

## The steps

1. Confirm the period and that the books are closed. If not, label the packet "preliminary".
2. Export the reports into one folder, or let Claude read them through a QuickBooks connection if you have one.
3. Agree once on 8 to 15 board-level lines (for example "Tithes and offerings"), so every packet compares the same way.
4. Claude builds the budget-versus-actual table and charts and flags lines that are far off budget.
5. Claude drafts a short explanation for each flagged line from the records or what you tell it, and writes "explanation needed" where it has no evidence.
6. Claude writes the summary: what happened, cash compared with your cash policy, restricted funds (money donors gave for a set purpose), and what the board must decide.
7. Send the draft to the treasurer. Claude works in every comment and shows what changed. Repeat until they call it final.
8. You or the board secretary send the final packet.

## What stays with people

The treasurer confirms every figure and explanation. The board makes the decisions. People decide what to share and with whom.

## Keep it safe

Use summary reports, not donor-level detail. Leave donor names out unless your practice is to name them. Show salaries only as totals. Never share QuickBooks passwords with any AI.

## Try it

Copy this into any AI chat, then paste or attach your reports:

> I prepare our ministry's financial report for the board. Here are our QuickBooks Online Profit and Loss and our budget for the same period. Group the lines into about ten categories, show budget, actual and variance, and flag anything more than 10 percent and 500 dollars off. Ask me what happened on each flagged line rather than guessing. Then draft a one-page plain-language summary, marked as a draft for our treasurer.
