# Do AI assistants know about your ministry?

## What this helps you do

People now ask AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity things like "where can I get groceries on Saturday?" This workflow checks whether those answers mention your ministry and get the facts right, then helps you fix what you can and re-check regularly.

## Who it's for

Pastors, directors, administrators and communications volunteers. No technical skill needed.

## What you'll need

- Your web address and confirmed facts: name, address, phone, times, what you offer.
- Three or four AI assistants (free accounts are fine) and a spreadsheet for results (a scorecard).
- Time: 60 to 90 minutes for the first check, one to three hours for fixes, about 20 minutes per re-check.

## The steps

1. Confirm a one-page fact sheet, so you can judge whether answers are right.
2. Write 10 to 15 questions real people ask, by city, by need, by type of ministry, and by your name.
3. Ask each assistant every question in a fresh chat. Record whether you were mentioned, whether it was accurate, and what sources it cited.
4. Have Claude summarize the scorecard and list fixes, most important first, each with an owner.
5. Make the fixes: correct wrong facts everywhere, write a clear About page and an FAQ page, add structured data (a small block of code stating your facts for machines), and match your name, address and phone on every listing.
6. Have Claude double-check the published fixes against your fact sheet.
7. Re-check with the same questions weekly for a month, then monthly. A scheduled task can help, if your plan includes it.

## What stays with people

A person confirms the fact sheet, your approver signs off on page changes, and each listing's owner updates it. Your leaders decide whether AI tools may read your site; Claude explains the trade-off.

## Keep it safe

Use only public information; never put a member's name or situation into a question. Sign in to each assistant and listing yourself, and never share passwords. Be realistic: answers change day to day, and no one can guarantee a mention.

## Try it

"Help me check whether AI assistants describe our ministry accurately. We are [name], a [type of ministry] in [city]; our website is [address]. First draft a fact sheet from our website for me to confirm. Then write 12 questions neighbors might ask, by city, need, ministry type and our name, and a scorecard table for each assistant's answers. Don't invent facts."

## Use the skill

Download the `ai-search-visibility` skill from https://github.com/johnbuck/brilliance-labs-discern-build and upload it in Claude's skills settings (Settings › Capabilities › Skills in Claude Desktop or claude.ai).
