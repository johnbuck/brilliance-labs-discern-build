# Make your sermons and study library searchable

## What this helps you do

Turn years of sermons, class notes, trainings or study resources into a private library you can ask, "what have we taught on grief?", and get answers with exact sources. It searches by meaning, so asking about burnout finds a sermon about being "stretched thin".

## Who it's for

Pastors, teachers and ministry staff with more material than anyone can remember. The small version needs no technical skill; the large one means installing one free app.

## What you'll need

- Your material as text: transcripts, PDFs or Word files. Audio needs transcripts first.
- Small library (up to a few hundred documents): a Claude Project, if your plan includes Projects. About 30 minutes.
- Large library (thousands of documents): Claude Code on your computer. An afternoon for a first test; more for a big library.

## The steps

1. Decide what you want to ask, and pick small (a Project) or large (on your computer).
2. List your collections, confirm you have the right to use each, and choose what to add first.
3. Gather the text; for the large version, Claude captures it on your computer.
4. Check nothing went missing: compare counts and spot-check samples against the originals.
5. Build it: upload files to a Project, or have Claude build a private index on your computer.
6. Ask questions. Claude finds the most relevant passages, then answers only from them, with sources (retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG).
7. Mark answers useful or not, so results improve.
8. Add new sermons and notes weekly, and save routines you repeat, like a research report on a Bible passage.

## What stays with people

You decide what goes in, what is preached or taught, and whether an answer is right. Check verses and quotes against the source before using them.

## Keep it safe

Take names out of personal stories; leave counseling notes out. Project files are stored by the provider; the large version keeps your library on your computer, off cloud syncing, and sends Claude only short excerpts. Index only what you own or have permission for: ask guest speakers, and capture licensed resources only through the app's own features. Fair use doesn't cover copying a whole licensed library; check the publisher's terms.

## Try it

"I'm sharing some of my sermon transcripts. Answer only from these files. For every point, cite the sermon title, date, speaker and paragraph, and quote exactly and briefly. If the files don't cover my question, say so. My first question: [your question]."

## Use the skill

Download the `searchable-library` skill from https://github.com/johnbuck/brilliance-labs-discern-build and upload it in Claude's skills settings (Settings › Capabilities › Skills in Claude Desktop or claude.ai).
